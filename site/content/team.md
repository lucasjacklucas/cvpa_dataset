The Canadian Voting and Policy Attitudes (CVPA) dataset was built by [Dave Armstrong](https://www.quantoid.ca/), [Jack Lucas](https://lucasjacklucas.github.io/), and Tyler Romualdi, with support from an SSHRC Insight Development Grant (430-2023-00595). Tyler Romualdi is the project director. During the dataset construction process, we received support, advice, and resources from [Sophie Borwein](https://politics.ubc.ca/profile/sophie-borwein/), [Elizabeth Goodyear-Grant](https://www.queensu.ca/politics/people/goodyear-grant-elizabeth), and [Zack Taylor](https://politicalscience.uwo.ca/people/faculty/full-time-faculty/zack-taylor.html). We are also very grateful to Holly Innanen and Russell Glenn for their excellent research assistance.
